# BHEM Project Charter

## Vision

Create an AI-assisted digital twin of the USS Barksdale that continuously monitors, analyzes, and optimizes home operations through automation, data analytics, and predictive intelligence.

---

## Mission

Reduce utility costs while increasing comfort through evidence-based decision making.

Transform disconnected smart-home systems into one unified computer capable of understanding the home as a complete system.

---

## Success Criteria

The project succeeds when:

- All major data sources are automatically collected.
- Daily updates require no manual intervention.
- AI recommendations are supported by measurable evidence.
- The system predicts electrical demand before APS peak hours.
- The dashboard explains why recommendations are made.
- The homeowner can quantify the financial impact of each strategy.

---

## Guiding Philosophy

Data → Information → Insight → Prediction → Action

The computer should evolve from reporting yesterday's events to recommending tomorrow's decisions.

---

## Long-Term Goal

Build a true Star Trek-inspired home computer that quietly understands the home and informs the captain only when action is needed.
